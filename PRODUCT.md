# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

A Fire TV viewer navigating a television from across the room with a remote.

## Product Purpose

NewTube is a personal SmartTube fork for discovering and watching YouTube content on TV, with a more modern YouTube-inspired interface and a separately installable identity.

## Operating Context

Fire TV Cube (Fire OS 7 / Android 9, 32-bit ARM) is the primary device. The app must work using directional navigation, selection, and Back.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve SmartTube's existing features, settings, playback, and account workflows. Install separately from the existing SmartTube application; existing SmartTube account and preference data are not automatically transferred. Updates should come from the owner's GitHub repository.

## Brand Commitments

Name: NewTube. A modern YouTube-inspired experience with a distinct identity.

## Product Principles

- Make content discovery and playback feel clear and immediate on a TV.
- Preserve the capabilities and settings users depend on.
- Keep remote focus and navigation predictable.
- Keep personal-fork updates independent of upstream binaries.
